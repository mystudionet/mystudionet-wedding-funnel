Questions couples ask

How much does wedding photography cost?

Packages start at $2,500 for 8 hours. Standard with a 10x10 album is $3,500, and Premium with 12 hours of coverage is $4,500.

Is the engagement session really included?

Yes, with every package. Essential and Standard include a 30-minute mini session on Long Island. Premium includes a full 1-hour session.

How many photos will we get?

There is no shot limit during your coverage. Every photo we deliver is color-corrected, and the photos in your album are fully retouched.

When will we receive our photos?

Your online gallery is delivered within 6 weeks of your wedding.

Which areas do you cover?

Nassau and Suffolk County, the Hamptons, New York City and New Jersey.

Can we add a wedding film?

Yes. We also create cinematic wedding films, and Photo + Film bundles are available. Ask us when we call.
