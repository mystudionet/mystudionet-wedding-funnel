## How much does a wedding videographer cost on Long Island in 2026?
On Long Island in 2026, most professional wedding videographers charge between **$2,000 and $7,500**. Below $2,000 you are usually looking at a side hustle or a student building a portfolio. Above $7,500 you are typically paying for large crews, same-day edits, or a big-name studio brand.

My own films start at **$3,500 for 8 hours** of cinematic coverage, with a premium option of up to 12 hours for larger weddings. Every package includes professional color grading, licensed music, and a cinematic highlight film.

## What drives the price of a wedding film?


- **Hours of coverage.** The single biggest factor. Getting ready to last dance is usually 8 to 12 hours.
- **Who is filming.** An experienced filmmaker who has seen 300+ weddings reads a room differently than someone on their fifth. You are paying for judgment, not just a camera.
- **The edit.** A highlight film takes 40 to 60 hours of editing when it is done with care. Rushed edits are cheaper for a reason.
- **Licensed music.** Proper licensing means your film can live on YouTube and Instagram without being muted or taken down. It costs the filmmaker real money, and it is worth every penny.
- **Deliverables.** A highlight film, a full ceremony edit, drone footage, raw files. Each adds editing time.

## What hidden costs should you ask about?
When you compare quotes, ask these three questions. They are where cheap packages quietly become expensive:


- **Is music licensed?** If not, your film may vanish from social media the day you post it.
- **Are travel fees included?** I serve all of Long Island and New Jersey with no surprises, but many studios add fees past a mileage radius.
- **How long is delivery?** Mine is 8 to 12 weeks because I refuse to rush an edit. If someone promises one week, ask what that edit will look like.

## Is a wedding film worth it?
I will say the quiet part out loud: photos capture how the day looked. Film captures how it sounded and felt. Your grandmother laughing during the toasts. Your partner's voice breaking during the vows. In ten years, that is what you will want back.

Whatever you budget for video, book someone whose work makes you feel something. Watch their films, not their ads.
