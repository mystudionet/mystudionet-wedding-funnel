10 Questions to Ask Your Wedding Videographer Before You Book

By Kursad Yonet · September 5, 2026 · 5 min read

After 300+ weddings I can tell you: most couples ask about price and availability, then stop. The questions below are the ones I wish every couple asked, because the answers reveal exactly who you are hiring.

1. Can I watch a full wedding film, not just highlights?

Anyone can cut 60 good seconds from 10 hours of footage. A full film shows how they handle dim receptions, rain, and running behind schedule.

2. Who is actually filming my wedding?

Some studios book you with the owner's portfolio and send a subcontractor. When you book me, you get me. Make sure the person whose work you loved is the person showing up.

3. What happens if you are sick or have an emergency?

The answer you want: a vetted network of trusted professionals who can step in. The answer you do not want: silence.

4. How do you handle audio during the ceremony and toasts?

Bad audio ruins more wedding films than bad video. A professional will talk about lapel mics, backup recorders, and pulling a feed from the DJ.

5. Is your music licensed?

Unlicensed music means your film can be muted or removed from social platforms. Licensed music is a mark of a real business.

6. What is your filming style, and how do you direct us?

My style is cinematic documentary. I capture what happens naturally instead of staging it. Whatever style you choose, make sure they can describe it clearly, because it decides how your day feels.

7. When will we get our film, and in what form?

Expect a specific answer: what you receive, in what resolution, delivered how, and by when. Mine is a color-graded highlight film within 8 to 12 weeks.

8. How many weddings do you take per season?

Someone filming three weddings every weekend is delivering someone's edit late. Limited bookings usually mean better films.

9. Do you know our venue?

Not a dealbreaker, but experience at your venue means they already know where the light falls at 5 pm and where the good backdrops hide. I have filmed at most major venues across Suffolk and Nassau County.

10. What do you need from us to do your best work?

This one flips the table. A professional has a real answer: a timeline conversation, ten minutes for couple portraits at golden hour, a seat near the toasts. If they have never thought about it, they are winging your wedding.

The pattern behind all ten

Every question above tests the same thing: has this person systematized their craft, or are they improvising? Your wedding day has no retakes. Book the one with answers.
